# Radius & shadows

Buttons and inputs use `rounded-md`, cards `rounded-xl`, dialogs `rounded-lg`.

## Shadows

Elevation is subtle: `shadow-xs` on buttons and inputs, `shadow-sm` on cards, `shadow-md` and up on
popovers, menus and dialogs.
